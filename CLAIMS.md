# CLAIMS

Locked language for the manuscript. The Scribe may not exceed this.

## We will claim (if tests pass)

1. The endocannabinoid system in humans is a small set of **cell states** (receptors + enzymes), visible in public single-cell data, not only “CB1 brain / CB2 immune” slogans.
2. At least some of those states can be distinguished from **B-cell / TLS abundance**.
3. A **frozen** state score, defined without ICI outcome labels, either **does** or **does not** associate with checkpoint **response** in IMvigor210 after B-cell/TLS/CD8 adjustment (Gate B Model 1). Overall survival is reported and is **not** the confirmatory pass. Melanoma GEO sets are **replication**, not the powered test.

## We will not claim

- Cannabis or CBD/THC is an anticancer therapy.
- CB2 **is** an immune checkpoint in the clinical sense (that needs causation).
- Patients should stop or start cannabis during Keytruda.
- Held-out melanoma n≈28 “validates a biomarker.”
- An OS-only signal, unadjusted-only signal, or deconvolved-fraction signal is a checkpoint association.

## Framing that stays even if ICI is null

A careful single-cell endocannabinoidome map is still the paper, with the ICI chapter reported as negative after confounders.
