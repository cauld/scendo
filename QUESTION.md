# QUESTION

**One sentence.** Where does the human endocannabinoid system actually sit at single-cell resolution, and do those cell states associate with immune-checkpoint response after you account for B cells?

**Why this question.** Cannabis during PD-1/PD-L1 therapy is clinically unsettled: some cohorts look worse on immunotherapy, mouse CB2 work looks like an immune brake, CBD in one CRC single-cell study looks like it can help PD-1 via macrophages, and in human tumors *CNR2* is often a **B-cell** gene (B cells already predict better ICI in several cancers). A map without the confound test is only a resource. An ICI signature without the map is another *CNR2* Kaplan–Meier.

**What this is not.** A test of whether cannabis treats cancer. A practice guideline. A causal proof that CB2 is a clinical checkpoint.

**Success for v1 (solo computational paper).** Named cell states that are not a relabeled B-cell score, plus a **frozen** test of those states on ICI transcriptomes the states were not trained on. Either a confound-resistant association or a clean negative is a complete paper.
